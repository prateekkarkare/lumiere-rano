Ruling 1 — volumetry is computed in ATLAS space

Atlas space (MNI 1 mm) is the reporting space. No Jacobian correction is applied, because none is needed.

The concern was that DeepBraTumIA's descriptor names neither the atlas nor the transform class, so if registration were affine or deformable, every reported mm³ would be scaled by a per-patient Jacobian determinant — invisible to Dice, invisible to an overlay, and fatal to every downstream RANO percentage.

Decided directly rather than inferred: all 2,396 .tfm files in the archive are rigid — 3×3 determinant 1.0000000000, orthonormal to 1.5e-15. A rigid transform cannot rescale volume. Confirmed by measurement: median native-vs-atlas deviation is 0.00% at every anisotropy and every lesion size, over 5,776 comparisons. Zero masks touch the atlas field-of-view boundary, so clipping — the other silent volume-loss path — is also empirically dead.

The brief's fallback branch would have been the wrong move.

The stated remedy for divergence was to report native-space back-transformed volumes instead. That assumes divergence means bias. What the data shows is noise: the native masks are the same segmentation resampled onto coarser grids, so they are strictly noisier, not truer. Switching the reported numbers to native space would add quantisation error and remove nothing. The correct amendment is neither branch of the original either/or — keep atlas, and attach a size-dependent uncertainty.

The error bar is a function of lesion size, not of compartment

A single tolerance would be wrong by roughly 20× across the range. "The enhancing rim is the fragile compartment" is a confound — enhancing compartments are simply the smallest; control for size and the three compartments converge. p10–p90 spread on ≥6× anisotropic grids (the conservative end):

compartment sizespreadconsequence
0.2–1 cm³36.6 pplarger than a RANO response threshold — a call could flip on grid choice alone
1–5 cm³14.8 ppmaterial; must be carried as uncertainty
5–20 cm³5.3 ppcomfortable
20–60 cm³2.8 ppcomfortable
> 60 cm³1.5 ppnegligible

On isotropic native grids the same bands are 1.9 / 0.9 / 0.4 / 0.2 / 0.1 pp. These numbers are encoded as VOLUME_UNCERTAINTY_PP in contract/data_contract.py and every emitted volume carries its own uncertainty_pp — a volume is never reported as a bare number.

Binding constraint on Piece 4: the RANO engine must carry size-dependent confidence, and any new-lesion rule needs a minimum-volume floor below which a "change" is not reported as real.

Ruling 2 — the DeepBraTumIA label decode was wrong and is corrected

Label 1 = contrast-enhancing. Label 2 = necrosis / non-enhancing. Label 3 = edema.

label_schema.py had 1 and 2 swapped, inherited from the Label name column of LUMIERE-pyradiomics-deepbratumia-features.csv, which is itself mislabeled — so the schema's "triangulation across three sources" in fact rested on one.

The evidence was already in hand and was resolved the wrong way. lumiere_eda.ipynb had derived the correct mapping empirically, by matching mask voxel counts to the JSON, and printed {1: 'Enhancing', 2: 'Necrosis', 3: 'Edema'} directly above the contradicting CSV table. The contradiction was seen, and closed with "use the 'Label name' STRING for volumetrics; the mask integers follow a different convention" — trusting the vendor's strings over the dataset's own numbers. The lesson is not "verify empirically", which was done; it is when two sources disagree, the one that can be checked against physics wins.

Three independent checks establish the true mapping: the shipped measured_volumes_in_mm3.json matches Enhancing_Core to the label-1 count in 599/599 atlas masks; label 1 takes up contrast (median normalised CT1−T1 +0.587, enhancing more than label 2 in 51/52 sampled cases) while label 2 loses signal (−0.073); and the resulting trajectories are clinically coherent. COMPOSITE_REGIONS["DeepBraTumIA"]["ET"] changed from {2} to {1}; TC and WT are unions and were unaffected.

Blast radius — narrower than it first looked

The ~42% Task-1 figure is NOT invalidated and does not need re-running.

That analysis read the shipped measured_volumes_in_mm3.json by key (Enhancing_Core), which was always the correct compartment. The swap only endangered code that reads MASK integers through label_schema.py — and this package's volumetry was the first such consumer, fixed before it shipped. Nothing already published or concluded is wrong. An earlier statement in this session that the M6c check "must be re-run" was mistaken.

Re-derivation from the mask, both ways

Not a repair of history — a measurement of what the bug would have cost, and a check that the mask-based path (which the pipeline will use) agrees with the JSON-based conclusion. A pure enhancing-volume rule (CR at zero; PD ≥ +40% vs nadir; PR ≤ −65% vs baseline; SD otherwise) over 372 assessable timepoints:

variantagreementCR recallPDPRSD
corrected (enhancing = label 1)55.9%57.7%78.3%35.0%2.2%
swapped (enhancing = label 2)51.1%7.7%70.6%25.0%18.7%

The CR column is the tell: complete response means the enhancing compartment vanishes, so watching the wrong label made CR nearly undetectable (7.7% → 57.7%, a 7.5× improvement). Caveat: this is a reimplementation of the rule, and it scores a different denominator than M6c did, so 55.9% is not directly comparable to the ~42% — only the corrected-vs-swapped delta under one identical rule is. The strategic conclusion is unchanged and reinforced: a single enhancing-volume signal is insufficient — SD recall is 2.2%, i.e. the rule essentially cannot recognise stable disease — so the four-signal design stands.

Ruling 3 — HD-GLIO-AUTO is an ENHANCING-ONLY second opinion

Only ET-vs-ET agreement is meaningful. HD-GLIO's non-enhancing class is not comparable to any single DeepBraTumIA compartment.

HD-GLIO-AUTO emits two classes. Its label 1 merges edema, non-enhancing solid tumour and necrosis into one "T2/FLAIR non-enhancing" class — necrosis does not enhance, so it lands there. That class therefore straddles two DeepBraTumIA compartments (2 and 3) and cannot be differenced against either. It also means HD-GLIO cannot form a tumour core at all.

Its enhancing class is clean, and its mapping was independently re-verified with the same contrast test that caught the DeepBraTumIA swap: label 2 enhances in 40/40 sampled cases (median +0.571) while label 1 does not (−0.034). HD-GLIO is correct as documented — the pyradiomics CSV happens to be right for this tool and wrong for the other, which is why per-tool verification is mandatory rather than optional.

Consequence: the QC gate is Dice(ETDBT, ETHDG) only. No label fusion, no whole-tumour comparison, and HD-GLIO is never a co-equal volume source.

Cohort lock

Selection criterion. A timepoint is assessable iff it has a usable DeepBraTumIA segmentation and an expert RANO response label in {CR, PR, SD, PD}. Pre-Op and Post-Op are excluded — they are surgical states, not response assessments, and would otherwise pad trajectories. A patient enters the cohort with ≥ 6 assessable timepoints, which is a trajectory-length requirement: fewer cannot exercise nadir / confirmation logic.

Screening 91 patients / 638 timepoints: 591 usable segmentations, 616 expert ratings, 560 in the intersection, 372 of those carrying a response label. Applying the threshold gives the locked cohort.

Practice arm: Patient-067, -031, -072, -032, -059, -054, -062. Held-out IDs are recorded in output/cohort/cohort_lock.json so the split is fixed and auditable, and are deliberately not reproduced here.

Split rule — deterministic, no RNG. Partition by whether the trajectory contains a CR, then within each stratum order by assessable count descending (ties by ID) and take every 4th patient into practice. The CR stratification is not cosmetic: an unstratified stride put all 22 CR timepoints in held-out, which would have left the practice arm unable to develop the complete-response branch at all. Both arms now carry all four response classes, and the held-out set holds 155 follow-ups — far past the ≥15 needed to score.

Held-out discipline: no trajectory, volume or rating of those 17 patients is inspected until the scoring run. Only aggregate label counts were read, and only to balance the split before any modelling.

Piece 1 — CLOSED

Piece 1 is declared closed as of this memo.

It delivers: an ingestion adapter behind a two-front-door seam, a lazy internal case contract, a fingerprinter, a grid-alignment validator, a volumetry stage that reproduces the vendor's own numbers exactly, a machine-readable data contract carrying volumes with uncertainty, and a locked cohort with a disciplined split. The single number the product emits has been audited and is defensible.

Explicitly descoped into Piece 2 rather than left as silent debt: the validator registry/runner, a dedicated capability-profile type, the router/policy layer, the config-driven contract object, the skull-strip sanity check, and the HD-GLIO ET Dice gate. These are real gaps, listed in the ledger above; none of them block registration or the RANO engine, which is why they travel rather than hold the gate.
