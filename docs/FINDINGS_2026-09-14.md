## 2. Rule bug — PR without a measurable baseline (012, 065)

RANO: disease that was never measurable (≥ 10 × 10 mm) cannot have a partial response; best is SD.

| Case | Baseline lesion (ruler) | This scan | Why the rule still said PR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 012 wk-048 | wk-000-2: **no lesion ≥ 20 mm³** (110 mm³ of specks) | no lesion ≥ 20 mm³ | ratio of specks = −82% |
| 065 wk-029 | wk-000-2: 20.0 × 17.5 ✓ — but **re-operation at wk-025**, where the largest piece is 17.2 × 9.0 ✗ | 11.4 × 4.2 | baseline never resets at surgery |

- **Root cause:** `rano.criteria` and `run_rano_calls.py` never import `rano.measurement`. The only
  measurability input is the doctor's own `NonMeasurableLesions` column — blank on both.
- **The 20 mm³ floor is not the measurability test.** It decides "lesion or speck". Measurable is a
  ruler test; a 956 mm³ lesion (065 wk-025) fails it at 17.2 × 9.0 mm.
- **Fix:** before allowing PR, require a lesion ≥ 10 × 10 mm on the baseline scan (ruler, 26-connectivity,
  20 mm³ floor). 012 needs only this. 065 also needs the **surgery reset** (adjudication Fix 3, still open).
  Test on the *baseline*, not the follow-up — shrinking below 10 mm is what a real response looks like.

## 3. T2/FLAIR progression (090, 012, 070)

- **What it is:** the FLAIR-bright region grows because tumour spreads without taking up contrast.
  The contrast ring can shrink at the same time. See `figures/patient090_t2_progression.png`:
  enhancing 2,728 → 84 mm³ while the FLAIR-bright region on the other side spreads (54,229 → 92,557 mm³).
- **Why it is off:** our only T2 number is DeepBraTumIA's *edema* label = every FLAIR-bright voxel,
  swelling included. Measured at +40% vs its own nadir it fires on:

  | Doctor said | CR | PR | SD | PD |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | T2 fires | **21/25 (84%)** | 13/19 | 34/86 | 167/219 (76%) |

  It fires more on complete responses than on progression. Turned on, it would flip 090, 012 and 070
  to PD — and break most CRs.
- **Rulebook difference, not a code bug:** the ratings are "according to RANO" (2010, includes T2).
  RANO 2.0 (2023) dropped T2 for enhancing tumours, except during anti-angiogenic therapy.
- **Lead, unproven:** 090's label 2 (*necrotic / non-enhancing*) rose 278 → 6,113 mm³. Label 2 also
  holds resection cavities, so it cannot be used as-is.

## 4. Segmentation — the mask is not ground truth (070)

- LUMIERE ships **no human-drawn masks**. Both masks are software output; the only answer key is the
  doctor's rating.
- **Patient-070** (`figures/patient070_missed_lesion.png`, visits registered):
  wk-019 nothing · **wk-044 bright nodule, doctor measures 12 × 10 mm, DeepBraTumIA 0 mm³, HD-GLIO-AUTO 0 mm³**
  · wk-061 28 × 22 mm ring at the same spot (both tools ≈ 35,000 mm³). The second-opinion tool fails the
  same way, so it cannot catch this kind of miss.
- **Zero-enhancing scans are the weak spot.** 62 scored scans report exactly 0 mm³ enhancing; the doctor
  called CR 15, SD 29, **PD 18**. Outside the held-out arm: 24 scans — CR 9, SD 10, PD 5.
- **Validated so far:** volume arithmetic, group-level volumes, two tools agreeing on large lesions.
  **Never validated:** individual outlines, and small or new lesions — the size RANO decisions turn on.
