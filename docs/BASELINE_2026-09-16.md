# Baseline eligibility — treatment dates in, starting point chosen

*Written 2026-09-16. Steps 1–3 of the measurability work in `FINDINGS_2026-09-14.md` §2. The gate
itself (step 4) is not built yet. The new rule is OFF by default.*

## The idea

Whether a scan may be the reference every later scan is measured against is **not a property of the
image**. It depends on when the scan was taken relative to surgery and radiotherapy, and an MR
header carries neither date. So treatment dates are a required INPUT, delivered with the scans.

## What was built

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Treatment record | `src/rano/contract/treatment.py` | Surgery weeks and radiotherapy start/end on the patient's own week axis. Each date is **recorded**, **assumed** (only with a written reason — an assumption with a blank reason raises), or **missing** (`None`). `surgeries=None` ("nobody told us") and `surgeries=()` ("there was none") are different facts. `Patient.treatment` defaults to nothing delivered. |
| LUMIERE rebuild | `src/rano/adapters/lumiere/treatment.py` | LUMIERE ships no treatment dates, so they are rebuilt from the rating file's **Pre-Op / Post-Op labels only** — never the response call, which is the answer key. Surgery = week of the first Post-Op scan after it; consecutive Post-Op scans with nothing between them are ONE operation (Patient-010: weeks 1, 1, 2). Radiotherapy = first operation **+4 → +10 weeks**, declared. Works on week NUMBERS: the ratings and the folders disagree on labels ("week-000" vs "week-000-2"), which is why the harness silently failed to find the post-operative scan for 5 patients. |
| Placing a scan | `src/rano/criteria/timeline.py` | `place_scan` returns one of: before surgery · after surgery · during radiotherapy · too soon after radiotherapy · **after radiotherapy** (the only one that may be a starting point) · unknown. `choose_baseline` takes the FIRST qualifying scan, never the best-looking one. |
| The switch | `scripts/run_rano_calls.py` | `--baseline post-op-label` (default, unchanged) or `--baseline post-rt` (the new rule, per-patient radiotherapy end). |

**Window edges.** RANO 2.0 puts the baseline ~4 weeks after radiotherapy ends. Read as a strict
3–5 week window it disqualifies most real follow-up (31 of 62 usable patients have their first rated
scan later than week 15), so the rule is a **floor** — at least 3 weeks past radiotherapy — and a
scan further out than 5 weeks still qualifies, flagged `late`.

**Contradictions are not patched.** A Pre-Op scan followed by an ordinary scan means an operation the
labels never dated (Patient-079) or a label on the wrong week (Patient-013). Those records are left
missing and listed by `LumiereAdapter.audit_treatment()`. 72 of 74 usable patients rebuild cleanly;
16 have two or more operations; the assumed radiotherapy end is week 10 for 39 patients and weeks
11–14 for 33 — it was week 10 for everyone before.

## What it does to the calls (74 usable patients)

| | |
|---|---|
| Starting point moved | **70 of 74 patients** |
| Scans that stop being scored | **70** (they now fall before the starting point) |
| Calls that change | **13** — 6 were right and became wrong, 7 were wrong either way, **none improved** |
| Agreement on scored scans | 64.7% → **62.9%** |
| Balanced accuracy (all 91, mrano_volumetric) | 48.6% → **42.1%** |

Mechanically: dropping the early scans removes the small early volumes that set the nadir, so later
growth no longer clears +40% and progression becomes stable disease. This is the adjudication's F6
("moving the baseline wins by shrinking the exam") measured directly — except here it does not even
win. One forbidden comparison does disappear: Patient-004 week-071 was measured against a nadir from
before a second operation.

**Patient-012 week-048, the case that started this**: reference moves from week-000-2 (110 mm³ of
post-surgical rim) to week-016 (2,508 mm³), and the call stays **PR** — −82% becomes −99%, both past
the −65% threshold. Choosing the right reference does not stop a response being called on 20 mm³ of
specks. That needs the measurability gate, and Patient-012's second operation at week 22 needs the
surgery reset.

**Left OFF by default on purpose.** Correct by the rulebook, worse by the numbers, and incomplete
without the other two changes. Re-measure all three together.
